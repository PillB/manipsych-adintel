Simple baselines already achieve silhouette 0.005 (≥ 0.1); deep clustering only achieves 0.032 (Δ +0.026). Deep clustering NOT justified — simpler baselines suffice.

Method: per-baseline silhouette on a 3,000-record subsample (full corpus would be expensive and is unnecessary for silhouette comparison). How It Works

The authorship verifier uses 5 independent signals combined into a weighted score:

  • Stylometry (50%): Character 4-5-gram TF-IDF cosine similarity. Captures idiolect — word-choice habits, spelling patterns, punctuation style.
  • Template signature (20%): Digit/URL-normalized Jaccard. Captures structural templates — two ads from the same template share structure even if words differ.
  • Lexical richness (15%): Type-token ratio similarity. Captures vocabulary diversity — a verbose writer and a terse writer differ here.
  • Structural signature (10%): Punctuation ratios, sentence length, all-caps ratio. Captures formatting habits.
  • Council label overlap (5%): Jaccard over technique labels. Softest signal — two ads with the same technique palette are weakly more likely to share a source, but this never decides alone.

Length-aware abstention: Below 15 tokens, the system returns INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. Between 15-60 tokens, confidence is ramped from 0.30 to 1.00. Above 60 tokens, full confidence.

Calibration: Platt scaling fitted on 400 pairs (200 positive, 200 negative). Brier score = 0.0034, ECE = 0.0525.

Term-Prevalence Comparison (R1–R4)

For each comparison population, every term row reports: outlier_count / outlier_denominator, outlier prevalence %, control_count / control_denominator, control prevalence %, Cohen's h effect size (with conventional label), 95% Wilson-style CI on the difference, two-sided z-test p-value, Benjamini–Hochberg FDR-adjusted q-value, and min-support flag (≥5 hits in both arms). Rows marked ★ are meaningfully different: q<0.05, |h|≥0.50, CI lower bound > 0, meets min-support.

Explicit Non-Difference Statement (R4)

When outliers are NOT meaningfully different, we say so. The comparison outlier_vs_all_non_outlier above is the most-stringent test (full-corpus control arm). Its verdict is reported verbatim from the aggregate statistics — if it reads NOT_MEANINGFULLY_DIFFERENT, that means no term meets the q<0.05 + |h|≥0.50 + CI>0 + min-support criteria. Outliers in this corpus differ structurally (geographic terms, structured-info sections) but not in their overall persuasion technique distribution. The outlier_vs_same_cluster_non_outlier comparison is more sensitive because it controls for cluster-level baseline.
